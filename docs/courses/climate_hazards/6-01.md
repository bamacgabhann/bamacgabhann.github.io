# Climate Hazards 6.01: Climate Hazard Assessment and Mapping : Report structure

Every report will be different, depending on the location / hazard(s) / risk(s), so it's not possible to give a single "one size fits all" template structure. A potential default structure might be:

1. Introduction (including location and rationale)
2. Exposure (hazards which may affect the location)
3. Vulnerability (who / what might be impacted)
4. Forecasting (what might be possible in terms of trying to anticipate future hazard events)
5. Potential adaption / mitigation (what might be possible to reduce the severity or consequences of future hazard events)
6. Conclusions

Hazard maps, if included, might fall into exposure or vulnerability or forecasting sections, depending on the kind of hazard map; data analysis might similarly be included in different sections depending on the data and analysis.

This should be seen as a default structure, and can be adapted depending on your topic.
