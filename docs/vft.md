# Virtual Field Trips

Fieldwork is by far the best way to learn about geography or earth science. Unfortunately, it's not always possible to bring everyone to the field - but if I can't bring you to the field, I can at least try to bring the field to you. 

These virtual field trips comprise collections of videos and photographs recorded on location, in order to give you some insight into and understanding of these areas, and the associated topics.
